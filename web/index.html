The problem

Answers live in many documents at once

The answer to a single question is rarely in one file. It is spread across policies, handbooks, circulars and reports, and a normal chatbot answers from memory, with no way to check it.

Policies & guidelinesRules, eligibility criteria, approval routes
Academic documentsHandbooks, syllabi, standards, notes
Reports & manualsAnnual figures, procedures, circulars
Why this is hard
Manual search is slowReading four documents to find one line
Relevant details get missedThe answer may sit in the file you skipped
AI can answer without evidenceFluent, confident, and sometimes invented
The source can't be checkedNo way to confirm where an answer came from

Features

Built on top of the pipeline

§

Citations you can open

Every claim carries [Doc, Page]. Click it to read the exact passage the answer came from.

✓

Answer audit

Splits an answer into claims and checks each one against its sources, with a faithfulness score.

✎

Document insights

A summary, likely FAQs and a short quiz for any document, written only from its text.

⟶

Timelines

Pulls every dated fact on a topic from all documents and lines them up in order, with sources.

⌀

Built-in refusal

When the documents don't hold the answer, it says so instead of guessing, unless you allow general knowledge.

◉

Voice questions

Ask out loud with the microphone; your chat history stays saved in your browser.

FAQ

Good questions

Which files can I add?

PDF, Word (.docx) and plain text files, up to 12 per library. Scanned images without a text layer can't be read in the web version.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Text is extracted in your browser and the library is stored there. Only the passages matched to your question are sent to the model, and nothing is kept on the server.

What happens when the answer isn't in my documents?

RAG.AI says the documents don't contain it. Turn on “Allow general knowledge” to get a clearly labelled answer from the model's general knowledge instead.

How is this different from the local version?

The local version (FastAPI + Ollama, in backend/) adds embeddings, reranking, a model jury, contradiction detection and a Telegram bot. The web version runs the core RAG flow, audit, insights and timelines on Qwen 2.5 14B.

Why might the model show as offline?

Qwen runs on a lab GPU reached through a tunnel. If that machine is off, the status shows “Model offline” and the workspace explains what happened.
